The long-term idea: a social platform for music criticism, where you keep a listening diary, rate albums, and rankings come from the community rather than an editorial team. Two prototypes explore that ground.

The first is a Next.js app focused on rap: album pages, ratings and reviews, profile pages and rankings. Reviews are stored locally, which is enough to test the rating flow but not to host a community.

The second plugs rating into a live stream: Twitch or TikTok chat messages vote in real time, an overlay shows the moving ranking, and a history keeps the sessions. This version taught the most about the product: a collective score in real time is more alive than a solitary review page.

Actual state: two prototypes, no user accounts, no shared database. The next step is to choose the common data model before writing another line.
