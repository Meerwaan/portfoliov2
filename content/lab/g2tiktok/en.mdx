G2 reads "Go to". Not one bot but a family: Python tools that share one way of working (Telegram control, `assets/` and `outputs/` folders, SQLite user and credit databases) and cover every step of vertical video.

```text
G2tiktokWork        idea + niche  →  script and hook (OpenAI)
                    → ElevenLabs voice (edge-tts as fallback)
                    → royalty-free B-roll (Pexels, Pixabay)
                    → word-level karaoke subtitles (faster-whisper)
                    → 1080×1920 render, beat-cut editing
G2Tiktok            long video → 9:16 clips, blurred background, Telegram Stars credits
G2TiktokAutopost    series publishing across several accounts (Selenium)
G2Tiktok-Live-inter live guessing game for TikTok Live, OBS overlay
G2Trading-safe      off video: Solana / Raydium trading bot, same Telegram base
```

Most of the work went into robustness rather than video: persisted task queues to resume after an interruption, render history, temporary file cleanup, two-gigabyte transfers over MTProto, per-user accounts and credits, niche configuration in JSON (voice, price range) to change formats without touching code.

The trading bot is the serious cousin of the family: detection of new Raydium pools over WebSocket, automated safety analysis (honeypot, mint authorities, holder distribution), paper or live execution, post-trade position management, and the same Telegram dashboard as the others. It is shown here for the mechanics, not as advice.

Actual state: the generation chain runs and has produced about sixty videos; the splitter and the publisher have served on real series. The missing link is the automatic hand-off from renders to the publisher, still done by hand.
