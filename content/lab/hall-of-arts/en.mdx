A team project born at ESIEE IT: an app that geolocates street art around you, tells the story of each work with text and audio, and lets you see it in augmented reality where it stands.

My part is the React Native mobile app: the map and geolocation, work pages with an audio player, photo and rating uploads to the API, then the augmented-reality view integrated with ViroReact. The project was picked up again in 2026 for a new version with a rebuilt back end.

It is shown here for what it is: a school project done as a team, currently paused, whose mobile part taught me what augmented reality really costs on heterogeneous phones.
